   - connection/read timeouts are tightened

2. Adds a first on-device speaker-recognition signal:
   - uses the enrolled administrator voice reference
   - records a short comparison sample on demand
   - performs local acoustic comparison
   - reports probable-owner / not-confirmed plus confidence
   - available from Administrator Identity and by saying “verify my voice”

Security note:
Speaker confidence is a personalization signal only. It does not replace the root PIN,
device credential, or Guardian authorization for privileged changes.
