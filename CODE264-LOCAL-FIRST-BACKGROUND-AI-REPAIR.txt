LUMI CODE264 — LOCAL-FIRST BACKGROUND AI REPAIR

VersionCode: 264
VersionName: 4.0.17-local-first-background-ai-r1

Purpose
- Keep normal conversation on the on-phone Fast Brain.
- Treat online provider health as background availability only, not as a foreground routing trigger.
- Escalate to OpenAI/remote only for explicit stronger-brain requests or clearly heavy work.
- Prevent overlapping AI health checks and bound automatic retries to three attempts (15s, 60s, 300s), then pause until a later stale/manual refresh.
- A failed foreground OpenAI request records the failure but does not start a retry storm.
- Integration Center separates CONFIGURED, AVAILABLE NOW, and LAST USED FOR A REPLY.
- Each routed reply records ai_last_used_provider, ai_last_used_at, and ai_last_route_reason for diagnostics.
- Foreground/resume health refresh is throttled to a five-minute stale window.

Guardian remains unchanged.
