LUMI CODE 291 — VOICE + ONLINE ROUTING R10

VersionCode: 291
VersionName: 4.0.44-voice-online-routing-r10

Changes
- Main-screen Listen explicitly exits keyboard/wake-only mode and owns voice input.
- While Listen/conversation mode is active, recognized speech is promoted to a user turn instead of being mislabeled ambient speech.
- Weather with no spoken city uses the phone's available last-known location and routes directly to the dedicated weather provider.
- Dedicated live providers retry once on transient failure.
- If all dedicated providers fail, Lumi makes one online reasoning fallback before returning a failure.
- Online route/status/provider results are recorded in diagnostics preferences.
- Fast Brain remains excluded from foreground conversation.

Goal
Fast, direct online retrieval for weather/news/market/current-data requests while preserving stable voice conversation.
